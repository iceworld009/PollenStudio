TRAINING WORKFLOW

What happens during training

  1. Freeze included images and labels into a dataset version.
  2. Train SSD using your chosen starting checkpoint.
  3. Export the trained TensorFlow model.
  4. Calibrate on training images and convert to 8-bit TFLite.
  5. Compile the detector for Coral Edge TPU.
  6. Measure precision and recall on validation images.
  7. Package the files and add the CPU model to your model selector.
